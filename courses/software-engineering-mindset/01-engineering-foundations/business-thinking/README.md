# Business Thinking

## Objective

Learn to think like a software engineer before writing code.

This module focuses on understanding businesses, identifying problems, analyzing workflows, and communicating effectively with clients. The goal is to develop the engineering mindset required to build software that solves real business problems.

---

# Learning Outcomes

After completing this module, you should be able to:

- Distinguish problems from proposed solutions.
- Understand how businesses operate before designing software.
- Conduct effective client discovery conversations.
- Gather business requirements using structured frameworks.
- Analyze business processes objectively.
- Identify root causes instead of symptoms.
- Break complex workflows into smaller activities.
- Identify bottlenecks within business processes.
- Create process maps.
- Model decision points and alternative workflows.

---

# Topics

## Topic 1 — Problem vs Solution ✅

Learn to separate the client's actual problem from the solution they suggest.

---

## Topic 2 — Understanding Businesses Before Software ✅

Learn to understand how a business operates before discussing technology.

---

## Topic 3 — Learning to Ask Better Questions ✅

Develop questioning techniques that uncover business requirements instead of assumptions.

---

## Topic 3.1 — Engineering Information Map ✅

Use a structured framework to identify the information required before making engineering decisions.

Topics covered:

- Process
- People
- Data
- Pain
- Cause
- Frequency
- Impact
- Constraints
- Goal
- Risks

---

## Topic 3.2 — Client Discovery Conversations ✅

Practice interviewing clients to understand their business before discussing software solutions.

---

## Topic 4 — Cause-and-Effect Thinking ✅

Learn to distinguish between:

- Symptoms
- Causes
- Effects

Practice tracing business problems back to their root causes.

---

## Topic 5 — Process Thinking ✅

Learn how engineers analyze business workflows through:

- Observation
- Decomposition
- Bottleneck Identification
- Process Mapping
- Decision Points
- Happy Path vs Alternative Paths
- Multiple Decision Branches

---

## Topic 6 — Swimlane Diagrams 🚧

Separate business responsibilities between:

- Customers
- Employees
- Systems
- External Services

Learn how professional engineers model collaboration across different participants.
