# Stakeholder Analysis

## Objective

Learn how to identify everyone affected by a software system before designing a solution.

---

# Definition

A stakeholder is any person, group, or organization that affects or is affected by a system.

Software is rarely built for only one person. A software engineer must understand everyone's needs.

---

# Why Software Engineers Need This

Different stakeholders have different goals.

Example:

Customer
• Wants fast service.

Cashier
• Wants easy checkout.

Manager
• Wants accurate reports.

Owner
• Wants higher profit.

If you only design for one stakeholder, the system may fail for everyone else.

---

# Stakeholder vs Actor

Actor

Someone who directly interacts with the system.

Examples

- Customer
- Cashier
- Student

Stakeholder

Anyone who has an interest in the system, even if they never use it.

Examples

- Owner
- Manager
- Government
- Investors

---

# Questions to Ask

Who uses the system?

Who owns the business?

Who makes decisions?

Who receives reports?

Who maintains the system?

---

# Bakery Example

Stakeholders

- Owner
- Baker
- Cashier
- Customer
- Supplier

Actors

- Cashier
- Baker
- Customer

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Only thinking about customers.

❌ Ignoring managers.

❌ Ignoring suppliers.

---

# Best Practices

✔ Identify every stakeholder first.

✔ Understand their goals.

✔ Understand their problems.

✔ Understand how they interact.

---

# Checklist

Before designing software ask:

□ Who uses it?

□ Who benefits?

□ Who maintains it?

□ Who makes decisions?

□ Who receives reports?

---

# Summary

Good software solves problems for every important stakeholder, not just the primary user.
