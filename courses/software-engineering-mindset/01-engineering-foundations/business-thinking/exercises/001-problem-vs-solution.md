# Exercise 001 — Problem vs Solution

## Objective

Practice separating a **business problem** from a **proposed software solution**.

The goal is to develop the habit of investigating what the business actually needs before deciding what software should be built.

---

# Core Principle

> A proposed solution is not automatically the real problem.

When someone says:

> "We need an app."

Do not immediately start designing the app.

Instead ask:

```text
Requested Solution
       ↓
What problem is this supposed to solve?
       ↓
What is actually happening?
       ↓
Why is it happening?
       ↓
What does the business need?
       ↓
Possible Solutions
```

# Mastery Check

You are ready to move forward when you can consistently:

- Identify a proposed solution.
- Identify the reported problem.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Identify missing information.
- Ask questions before designing software.
- Avoid assuming the first explanation is the root cause.
- Consider multiple possible causes.
- Consider multiple possible solutions.
- Explain why a requested feature may not be the actual business need.

---

# Final Mental Model

```text
Client Request
      ↓
Is it a solution?

Identify the Problem
      ↓
Separate Facts from Assumptions
      ↓

      ↓
Investigate
      ↓
Understand the Real Need
      ↓
Consider Possible Solutions
```
