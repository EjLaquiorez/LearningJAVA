# Exercise 002 — Understanding Businesses Before Software

## Objective

Practice understanding a business before thinking about software.

The goal is to develop the ability to understand:

- What the business does
- What value it provides
- Who is involved
- How work happens
- Where problems occur

---

# Core Principle

> Understand the business before designing the software.

Do not begin with:

```text
What software should we build?
```

Begin with:

```text
What does this business actually do?
        ↓
Who is involved?
        ↓
How does the work happen?
        ↓
What information is involved?
        ↓
Where does the process experience problems?
```

---

# Mastery Check

You are ready to move forward when you can consistently:

- Describe what a business actually does.
- Identify the value the business provides.
- Identify the people involved.
- Describe the major business activities.
- Understand the current workflow.
- Identify important business information.
- Identify where problems occur.
- Distinguish business understanding from software design.
- Ask questions before proposing features.
- Explain why different businesses require different solutions.

---

# Final Mental Model

```text
Understand the Business
        ↓
Identify the Value
        ↓
Identify the People
        ↓
Understand the Process
        ↓
Identify the Information
        ↓
Observe the Problems
        ↓
Investigate the Causes
        ↓
Understand the Business Need
        ↓
Only Then Consider Software
```
