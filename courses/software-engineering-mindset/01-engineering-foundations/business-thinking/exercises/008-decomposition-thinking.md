# Exercise 008 — Decomposition Thinking

## Objective

Practice breaking a complex business process into smaller, understandable activities.

The goal is to develop the ability to move from:

```text
Large / vague process
        ↓
Smaller activities
        ↓
Specific steps
        ↓
Understandable workflow
```

---

# Core Principle

> If a process is difficult to understand as a whole, break it into smaller parts.

Do not describe a process only as:

```text
Customer buys something.
```

Decompose it:

```text
Customer selects item
        ↓
Customer brings item to checkout
        ↓
Employee scans item
        ↓
System calculates total
        ↓
Customer provides payment
        ↓
Payment is verified
        ↓
Transaction is completed
        ↓
Receipt is provided
```

The goal is not to create unnecessary detail.

The goal is to create **enough detail to understand what actually happens**.

# Mastery Check

You are ready to move forward when you can consistently:

- Break a large process into smaller activities.
- Identify parent and child activities.
- Turn vague descriptions into specific steps.
- Identify missing activities.
- Identify decisions within a process.
- Identify alternative and failure paths.
- Identify information involved in each step.
- Identify people involved in activities.
- Choose an appropriate level of detail.
- Avoid both under-decomposition and over-decomposition.
- Explain why decomposition should happen before deeper process analysis.
