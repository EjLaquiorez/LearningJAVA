# Exercise 003 — Learning to Ask Better Questions

## Objective

Practice identifying missing information and turning unknowns into useful investigation questions.

The goal is not to ask as many questions as possible.

The goal is to ask the **right questions** to reduce uncertainty and improve understanding of the business.

---

# Core Principle

> Better questions come from identifying what you do not know.

Do not begin with:

```text
What features should we build?
```

Begin with:

```text
What do we know?
        ↓
What do we not know?
        ↓
What information is important?
        ↓
What should we investigate?
        ↓
What question will reveal that information?
```

# Mastery Check

You are ready to move forward when you can consistently:

- Identify what is known.
- Identify what is unknown.
- Recognize which unknowns matter.
- Turn unknowns into useful questions.
- Ask about the current process before proposing features.
- Ask neutral questions instead of leading questions.
- Investigate people, process, data, causes, frequency, impact, goals, and constraints.
- Prioritize important questions.
- Avoid asking questions simply because they sound technical.
- Explain why a question is useful.
