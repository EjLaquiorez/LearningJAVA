# Exercise 007 — Observation Before Questions

## Objective

Practice observing a business process before asking questions or forming conclusions.

The goal is to develop the habit of separating:

```text
FACTS
vs
HYPOTHESES
vs
INVESTIGATIONS
```

You should learn to notice:

- Activities
- Waiting
- Repetition
- Delays
- Manual work
- Decisions
- People
- Information movement

---

# Core Principle

> Observe before you conclude.

Do not begin with:

```text
I think the problem is...
        ↓
Let's ask questions that confirm it.
```

Instead:

```text
Observe
   ↓
Record Facts
   ↓
Identify Unknowns
   ↓
Form Hypotheses
   ↓
Ask Questions
   ↓
Investigate
   ↓
Understand
```

---

# Mastery Check

You are ready to move forward when you can consistently:

- Observe a process without immediately judging it.
- Record facts separately from interpretations.
- Identify activities.
- Identify waiting and delays.
- Identify repetition.
- Identify manual work.
- Identify decisions.
- Track information movement.
- Recognize possible hypotheses.
- Avoid treating hypotheses as facts.
- Identify what requires further investigation.
- Ask questions based on observations.
- Explain why observation should happen before solution design.
