# Exercise 004 — Client Interview Questions

## Module

Phase 1 → Module 1 → Part A — Business Thinking

---

# Objective

Practice gathering business information before discussing software solutions.

---

# Scenario 1 — Bakery

Client Statement:

> "Business has been difficult lately. I think I need a mobile app."

### Information to Discover

- How the bakery currently operates
- What business problems exist
- Current business limitations
- What success means to the owner

---

# Scenario 2 — Coffee Shop

Client Statement:

> "Customers keep complaining that their orders take too long. I think we need self-order kiosks."

### Goal

Ignore the proposed solution.

Investigate the business instead.

---

# Scenario 3 — Hospital

Client Statement:

> "Nurses spend too much time writing on paper. We need tablets."

### Goal

Understand the workflow before discussing technology.

---

# Scenario 4 — Restaurant

Client Statement:

> "Customers leave bad reviews because food arrives late. We need a mobile app."

### Goal

Identify the process, the business pain, the constraints, and the desired outcome.

---

# Reflection

A software engineer's responsibility is not to immediately agree with a client's proposed solution.

Instead, the engineer should first understand:

- How the business works
- What problem exists
- What limitations exist
- What success looks like

Only after gathering sufficient information should possible solutions be discussed.
