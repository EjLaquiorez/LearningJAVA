# Exercise 005 — Client Discovery Conversations

## Objective

Practice conducting a structured conversation with a client or stakeholder to understand a business problem before proposing software.

The goal is to learn how to:

- Understand the current process
- Clarify business rules
- Discover pain points
- Investigate possible causes
- Identify constraints
- Understand goals
- Separate facts from assumptions
- Ask useful follow-up questions

---

# Core Principle

> A client conversation is an investigation, not a feature collection exercise.

Do not turn the conversation into:

```text
Client says feature
        ↓
Engineer writes feature
        ↓
Build software
```

Instead:

```text
Client Statement
       ↓
Clarify
       ↓
Understand Current Process
       ↓
Identify Pain
       ↓
Investigate Causes
       ↓
Understand Rules and Constraints
       ↓
Clarify Goal
       ↓
Identify Business Need
       ↓
Consider Solutions
```

# Mastery Check

You are ready to move forward when you can consistently:

- Start a client conversation without jumping to features.
- Understand the current process.
- Identify the people involved.
- Discover important information and data.
- Identify pain points.
- Investigate possible causes.
- Clarify business rules.
- Discover constraints.
- Clarify measurable goals.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Avoid leading questions.
- Identify remaining unknowns.
- Summarize the business problem clearly.
- Explain when you have enough information to begin discussing solutions.
