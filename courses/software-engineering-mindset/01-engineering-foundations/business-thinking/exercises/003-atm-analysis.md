# Case Study 02 — ATM Business Analysis

## Module

Phase 1 → Module 1 → Business Thinking

---

# Objective

Learn to view an ATM as a business process instead of a collection of screens.

---

# Business Scenario

A customer wants to withdraw money from an ATM.

The goal is to understand the complete business workflow before discussing software.

---

# Initial Workflow

```text
Customer inserts card
        ↓
ATM reads card
        ↓
Bank verifies account
        ↓
(To be completed during Topic 2 exercise)
```

---

# Actors

- Customer
- ATM
- Bank
- Banking System

---

# Business Goal

Allow customers to securely access banking services without visiting a bank branch.

---

# Engineering Thinking Questions

- What happens after the bank verifies the account?
- What information is exchanged?
- What could go wrong?
- What business rules exist?
- How does the bank protect customer money?

---

# Student Exercise

Complete the workflow after account verification.

Then identify possible failure scenarios.

---
