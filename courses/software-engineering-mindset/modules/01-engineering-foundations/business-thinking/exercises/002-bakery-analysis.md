# Case Study 01 — Bakery Business Analysis

## Module

Phase 1 → Module 1 → Business Thinking

---

# Objective

Understand how a business operates before thinking about software.

---

# Business Scenario

A neighborhood bakery sells bread to customers every day.

There is currently no discussion about software.

The goal is to understand the business itself.

---

# Actors

- Customer
- Baker
- Cashier
- Manager / Owner

---

# Activities

## Customer

- Enters the bakery
- Looks at available bread
- Chooses products
- Pays
- Receives bread
- Leaves

---

## Baker

- Prepares dough
- Bakes bread
- Restocks shelves
- Reports low ingredients

---

## Cashier

- Greets customers
- Calculates total
- Accepts payment
- Gives change
- Prints receipt
- Records sales

---

## Manager

- Reviews sales
- Monitors inventory
- Orders ingredients
- Handles business operations

---

# Information Used

- Bread Name
- Price
- Quantity
- Payment Amount
- Receipt Number
- Inventory Quantity
- Daily Sales

---

# Business Goal

Provide fresh baked goods to customers efficiently while generating profit for the business.

---

# Engineering Observations

This business can operate without software.

Software would only improve efficiency, accuracy, and reporting.

The business process exists independently of technology.

---

# Lessons Learned

Before designing software, engineers first understand:

- Who performs the work
- What activities occur
- What information is exchanged
- What the business is trying to achieve

---

# Mentor Feedback
